I am currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Biotechnology at BRAC University, Bangladesh. I am a graduate biotechnology researcher with a passion for understanding complex biological data and turning messy biological information into clearer answers with the help of both wet-lab and dry-lab work.

About

Overview

My academic background includes Fisheries and Biotechnology, with training in microbiology, molecular biology, genomics, and computational biology. I completed my undergraduate degree at Bangladesh Agricultural University and am currently pursuing my master's degree at BRAC University.

My research interests have developed around understanding microbial genomes, antimicrobial resistance, bacterial evolution, and the application of computational approaches to biological research.

My computational projects use publicly available genomic and transcriptomic datasets. I also have practical laboratory experience involving molecular biology, bacterial culture, fish dissection, and biological specimen examination.

Research Interests

Primary: microbial comparative genomics, AMR, bacterial genome evolution, pangenomics, virulence-associated genomic features, and mobile genetic elements.

Additional: computational biology, reproducible workflows, bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, functional genomics, and natural-product bioinformatics.

Research Experience

Master's Thesis Researcher

BRAC University, Bangladesh · M.Sc. in Biotechnology · 2024–Present

Supervisor: Dr. Mahdi Moosa · Status: Ongoing

My master's research investigates genomic variation and the distribution of antimicrobial-resistance, plasmid-associated, and virulence-associated features across publicly available Aeromonas genomes.

  • Comparative analysis of bacterial genomic datasets, genomic annotations, and genome organization.
  • Investigation of resistance- and virulence-associated sequences and mobile genetic elements.
  • Comparison of plasmid-associated and chromosomal genomic locations across four Aeromonas species.
  • Computational interpretation of genomic similarities and differences; thesis results are in progress.

Junior Research Collaborator

ABCD Laboratory · Advanced Bioinformatics, Computational Biology and Data Science Laboratory · 2025–Present

Contributing to literature-based natural-product investigation and structured data curation associated with Nypa fruticans.
